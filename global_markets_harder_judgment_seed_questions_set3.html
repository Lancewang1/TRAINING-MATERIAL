20 Harder Judgment-Call Seed Questions

Designed one level above the previous decision-first set. These tasks require the agent to resolve competing contractual clauses, select the correct benchmark or instrument, attribute causality, or test a plausible alternative interpretation—while preserving an objectively replayable answer.

Rule precedenceFallbacksPath dependenceBenchmark selectionDelivery optionalityCredit definitionsCounterfactual checks

What makes this set harder?

The model is not merely asked whether a number is above or below a threshold. It often has to decide which number, date, benchmark, legal entity, futures contract, settlement convention, or payoff branch is the relevant one before it can perform the calculation.

competing rules → identify the controlling rule → retrieve the right evidence → make the judgment → calculate → verify that a plausible wrong interpretation would lead elsewhere

That creates more genuine financial judgment without giving up ground truth. Tasks involving purely discretionary issuer decisions or unspecified pricing models are deliberately excluded unless the relevant decision or assumptions are supplied as evidence.
